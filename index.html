Billie Eillish

American Singer, Songwritter, Grammy Winner

Billie Eilish Pirate Baird O’Connell was born on December 18, 2001, in Los Angeles, California. She spent her childhood growing up in an environment that was surrounded by music and creativity; she was homeschooled along with her older brother, FINNEAS, who is her best collaborator and producer.

She posted her debut single, "Ocean Eyes," online, which was written and produced by FINNEAS. It went viral in no time and thrust her into the spotlight as a teenager.

Her debut album, “When We All Fall Asleep, Where Do We Go?”, was a huge critical and commercial success, cementing her as one of the most distinctive new voices in pop music.

With her whispery vocals, genre-bending production and a daring, ever-changing sense of style, she would soon become one of the defining figures of modern pop culture.

And beyond music, Billie is known for her openness about mental health and body image, and using her platform to speak out on causes such as climate action.

Over the years, she has won numerous Grammy Awards and continues to push her sound in new directions while staying true to the raw, personal songwriting that first made her a star.
